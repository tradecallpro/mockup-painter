5 Signs Your Bakersfield Home Is Ready for an Exterior Repaint

Sample post for illustration. Not written by The Paint Doctor.

Your exterior paint works quietly in the background until it doesn't. Bakersfield sun is hard on a finish. If you own a home here, here are five signs it may be time to have a painter take a look.

1. Peeling, cracking, or bubbling paint

Once the film breaks, moisture gets under it and the damage spreads. Peeling on one wall usually means the same coat is failing on the others.

2. Color that has faded unevenly

South and west walls take the most sun in Kern County. When they look washed out next to the shaded sides, the coating has thinned past protecting the surface.

3. Cracked caulk and hairline stucco cracks

Failed caulk around windows and doors lets water in. Small stucco cracks are normal, but they should be sealed and coated before they grow.

4. Exposed wood or soft spots

Bare or soft fascia and trim will not hold paint, and they rot fast. Catching them at repaint time costs a fraction of replacing the wood later.

5. A chalky film on your hand

Run a hand along a sunny wall. If it comes away chalky, the paint is breaking down. New paint will not stick well until that surface is washed and prepped.
